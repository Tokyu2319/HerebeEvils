A perspective where one’s understanding of magic coincidentally aligns with the underlying truths of reality.  
  
Rather than interpreting magic through mythology, imposed systems, or incomplete research, individuals within this perspective arrive at understandings that closely resemble the fundamental principles described throughout this Textbook.  
  
Such understanding is often incomplete or specialized. An individual may correctly understand certain aspects of reality such as [the universe](../../01%20The%20Laws%20of%20Reality/01%20The%20Universe.md), [beings](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md), [mana](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/01%20Thaumaturgy/01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md), while lacking understanding in other areas.  
  
This perspective does not necessarily imply complete mastery of magic, but a more accurate understanding of reality than conventional interpretations.
