Magic perceived as an unknowable, supernatural, or fictional phenomenon.  
  
This perspective is common in modern settings where magic is poorly understood, hidden from the public, dismissed as fantasy, or feared as something irrational. Magical phenomena are often interpreted through myths, folklore, superstition, religion, urban legends, or conspiracy rather than systematic study.  
  
This perspective emphasizes mystery, uncertainty, and the inability to fully explain magical events within conventional understanding.
