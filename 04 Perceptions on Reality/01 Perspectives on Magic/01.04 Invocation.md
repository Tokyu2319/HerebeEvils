A method of magic where power is drawn from an external source or reservoir.  
  
Practitioners channel [mana](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/01%20Thaumaturgy/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md) through faith, contracts, blessings, conduits, or direct connections to more powerful entities, structures, or reservoirs.  
  
The user does not necessarily generate or fully understand the power they wield, as the invoked source often exists beyond their direct control or comprehension.  
  
Invocation may exist independently or alongside systems imposed through [world frameworks](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/%E7%B5%82%20Thaumic%20Collection/World%20Framework.md), particularly within worlds governed by higher authorities or structured magical systems.
