There’s two distinct types of memories, the [vessel](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md)’s memory and the [mind](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.02%20Mind.md)’s memory.  
  
The vessel’s memory is the capacity to store and recall information. connecting synapses and data in a hardware are information inside a vessel’s cognition.  
  
The mind’s memory is what I define as a being’s [identity](./01%20Identity*.md). **During the process of interpreting [experiences](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/02%20Experience.md), the mind will form memories in itself as a byproduct**. These memories are separate from the vessel’s memories as they can be manipulated by [manon](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/01%20Thaumaturgy/01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/03%20Manon.md).  
  
The memories of both aspects are lost when those aspects are destroyed, naturally, the mind will die out when there’s no compatible vessel. **Recovering a soul’s mind and its memories is possible due to the existence of [resurrection magic](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/%E7%B5%82%20Thaumic%20Collection/Resurrection%20Magic.md).**
