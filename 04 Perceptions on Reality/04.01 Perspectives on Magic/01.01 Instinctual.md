Magic used without formal understanding, often discovered accidentally or developed through instinct.  
  
This perspective is most commonly observed in simple [beings](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) such as plants, animals, and primitive organisms. These beings may naturally manipulate [mana](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/03.01%20Thaumaturgy/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.01%20Mana.md) or magical phenomena without comprehending the underlying principles behind their actions.  
  
The use of magic in this perspective is reactive, evolutionary, or biological rather than intellectual or systematic.  
  
Instinctual perspectives may continue to exist even within highly advanced civilizations.
