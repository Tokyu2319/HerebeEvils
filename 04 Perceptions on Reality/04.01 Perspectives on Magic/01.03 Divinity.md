Magic defined, restricted, or structured by a higher being or authority.    
  
In this perspective, sapient beings access magic through systems intentionally designed by gods, ascendant [beings](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md), or other superior entities. These systems may impose rules, classifications, permissions, limitations, or artificial abstractions upon magical usage.  
  
Such systems are often maintained through large-scale [enchantments](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/03.01%20Thaumaturgy/02%20Structural%20Thaumics/02.01%20Enchantments.md) such as [world frameworks](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/03.03%20Thaumic%20Collection/World%20Framework.md), particularly [system engines](../../03%20General%20Thaumics/03.03%20Thaumic%20Collection/World%20Framework.md#System%20Engine), which regulate how magic is used or how reality itself is perceived.  
  
This perspective is commonly represented through “game-like” magical systems found in <!--trashy--> isekai narratives, where magic behaves according to predefined mechanics similar to software designed by a developer.
