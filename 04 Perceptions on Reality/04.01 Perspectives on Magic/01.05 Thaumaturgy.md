Magic understood as a systematized, reproducible, and researchable force.  
  
Rather than being treated as mysticism, Thaumaturgy approaches magic as a formal science governed by observable principles, experimentation, engineering, and repeatable methodologies.  
  
Practitioners of Thaumaturgy often study magic academically and develop technologies, infrastructure, or disciplines around its predictable behavior. However, understanding may vary greatly between civilizations, organizations, or individuals, and many aspects of magic may remain theoretical, disputed, or entirely unknown.
