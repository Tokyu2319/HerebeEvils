Type: Defense  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to provide rapidly deployable emergency protection through layered armana structures.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) may reproduce the structural properties of multiple conventional materials within a single cohesive assembly. By combining structures optimized for hardness, toughness, flexibility, impact absorption, and load distribution, composite barriers provide temporary protection against threats that cannot be avoided, intercepted, or otherwise mitigated.  
  
Structure:    
Composite Barriers consist of multiple armana layers arranged to perform complementary defensive functions. Outer layers commonly prioritize hardness and resistance to penetration, while internal layers distribute impact forces and absorb structural stress. The barrier may be formed as a protective panel, temporary armor, enclosed shelter, or directional defensive screen depending on operational requirements.  
  
Functionality:  
- Provides immediate physical protection  
- Resists penetration and fragmentation  
- Distributes and absorbs impact forces  
- Creates temporary cover during emergencies  
- Protects against attacks from configurable directions  
- Enables rapid deployment of defensive structures  
  
Limitations:  
- Requires mana expenditure to construct and maintain  
- Large barriers may contribute significantly to [mana exhaustion](../../../../02%20Thaumic%20Principles/03%20Mana%20Exhaustion.md)  
- Excessive damage may induce structural failure  
- Protection is limited by structural complexity and available mana  
- Sustained attacks may overwhelm insufficiently reinforced barriers  
- Unsuitable for continuous deployment in most circumstances  
  
Applications:  
- Emergency personal protection  
- Temporary armor  
- Floating defensive panels  
- Defensive enclosures  
- Portable cover  
- Emergency fortifications  
- Protective bulkheads  
- Hazard shielding  
  
Notes:    
Composite Barriers are frequently suspended or positioned through [manon](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/03%20Manon.md) manipulation, allowing practitioners to rapidly interpose protection without occupying their hands. Due to the substantial mana expenditure associated with construction and maintenance, Composite Barriers are commonly employed as emergency defensive measures when conventional equipment, defensive enchantments, or other protective methods prove insufficient.
