Type: Structural Component  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to transfer electrical current between electrical devices, systems, and structures.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) reproduces the conductive pathways of electrically conductive materials through cohesive configuration. Because conductivity emerges from structural permissiveness rather than energetic chemical reactions, armana structures may transmit electrical current while remaining resistant to oxidation and electrochemical degradation.  
  
Structure:    
Electrical Conduits consist of continuous conductive armana pathways configured to maintain uninterrupted charge transfer. The structures may be formed as wires, cables, traces, or other conductive routes depending on application requirements. High structural continuity is required to preserve conductivity across the entire conduit.  
  
Functionality:  
- Conducts electrical current  
- Transfers electrical energy  
- Connects electrical systems  
- Supports electrical circuitry  
- Enables lightweight electrical infrastructure  
- Resists corrosion and oxidation  
  
Limitations:  
- Cannot generate electricity intrinsically  
- Structural discontinuities interrupt conductivity  
- Damaged sections may decohere into bonded armana  
- Cannot reproduce electrochemical energy storage  
- Requires external electrical sources for operation  
  
Applications:  
- Electrical wiring  
- Power distribution networks  
- Circuit connections  
- Electromagnetic systems  
- Industrial machinery  
- Communication systems  
- Thaumic-technological infrastructure
