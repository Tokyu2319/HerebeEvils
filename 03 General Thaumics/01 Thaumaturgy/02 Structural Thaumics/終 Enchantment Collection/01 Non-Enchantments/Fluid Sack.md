Type: Fluid Container  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to contain, transport, and store liquids through flexible armana structures.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) may reproduce the cohesive properties of flexible membranes capable of maintaining enclosed volumes. By forming continuous sealed surfaces, armana structures can contain fluids while remaining lightweight and deformable.  
  
Structure:    
Fluid Sacks consist of thin cohesive armana membranes arranged into enclosed cavities. The structure is configured to resist fluid leakage while permitting deformation under changing volume and pressure. Reinforcement patterns may be incorporated to improve durability and increase containment capacity.  
  
Functionality:  
- Stores liquids  
- Transports fluids  
- Conforms to available space  
- Reduces reliance on rigid containers  
- Enables temporary fluid reservoirs  
- Supports integration with larger thaumical systems  
  
Limitations:  
- Vulnerable to puncture and structural damage  
- Excessive internal pressure may rupture the membrane  
- Structural failure results in fluid leakage  
- Cannot contain fluids exceeding the cohesion limits of the structure  
- Requires continuous structural integrity to remain sealed  
  
Applications:  
- Water storage  
- Alchemical reagent containment  
- Medical fluid reservoirs  
- Hydraulic systems  
- Portable fluid transport  
- Temporary storage vessels  
- Thaumical apparatus  
- Emergency supply containers
