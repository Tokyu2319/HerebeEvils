Type: Structural Component  
  
Purpose:  
Non-enchantments designed to provide long-term structural support in aquatic and chemically aggressive environments while resisting corrosion and environmental degradation.  
  
Principle:  
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) reproduces the structural properties of conventional materials through cohesive configuration rather than energetic atomic interactions. Because armana lacks the energetic states required for oxidation, corrosion, and electrochemical degradation, marine structures may maintain their integrity despite prolonged exposure to seawater and other chemically hostile environments.  
  
Structure:  
Marine Frameworks consist of interconnected armana beams, ribs, supports, and reinforcement members configured to distribute loads while maintaining cohesion under continuous environmental exposure. Structural continuity is prioritized to minimize weak points and improve resistance to mechanical stress.  
  
Functionality:  
- Supports long-term marine construction  
- Resists corrosion and oxidation  
- Maintains structural stability during prolonged seawater exposure  
- Reduces maintenance requirements  
- Enables deployment in chemically hostile environments  
- Provides durable support for aquatic infrastructure  
  
Limitations:  
- Remains vulnerable to mechanical damage  
- Structural failure may occur under excessive loads  
- Does not reproduce energetic chemical behaviors  
- Cohesion failure may result from severe thaumical disruption  
- Requires sufficient reinforcement for high-pressure environments  
  
Applications:  
- Ship frameworks  
- Submarine hull frameworks  
- Underwater habitats  
- Offshore platforms  
- Aquatic infrastructure  
- Docks and piers  
- Oceanic research facilities  
- Long-term submerged structures
