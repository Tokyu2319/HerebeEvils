Type: Structural Component  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to provide lightweight load-bearing structures for transportation, mobility, and large-scale construction.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) reproduces the structural properties of physical materials while lacking much of the intrinsic energy contributing to the mass of conventional matter. As a result, armana frameworks possess significantly reduced density while retaining useful mechanical strength.  
  
Structure:    
Lightweight Frameworks consist of interconnected armana beams, lattices, ribs, and support members arranged to distribute loads efficiently while minimizing structural mass. Reinforcement patterns are commonly incorporated to improve rigidity and resistance to deformation.  
  
Functionality:  
- Supports structural loads  
- Reduces overall construction mass  
- Improves buoyancy and mobility  
- Lowers transportation requirements  
- Enables large lightweight structures  
- Allows temporary deployment of oversized frameworks  
  
Limitations:  
- Reduced mass may decrease resistance to external forces  
- Large frameworks are vulnerable to cohesion failure if insufficiently reinforced  
- Cannot reproduce energetic material behaviors  
- Excessive structural discontinuity may trigger decoherence  
  
Applications:  
- Airship frames  
- Aircraft structures  
- Ship superstructures  
- Portable buildings  
- Deployable bridges  
- Mobility equipment  
- Temporary platforms  
- Structural skeletons
