Type: Defense  
  
Purpose:    
An enchantment designed to protect the user’s head from physical attacks through the controlled application of [manon](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/03%20Manon.md).  
  
Principle:    
Upon activation, the enchantment gathers manon from the user’s existing manon field or converts supplied [mana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md) into additional manon. The resulting force is maintained around the head and may be directed to repel, deflect, or slow incoming physical threats before impact.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of manon manipulation scripts embedded within a compact equipment, commonly taking the form of a necklace, circlet, helmet component, or similar piece of equipment. The enchantment is configured to project defensive forces around the user’s head.  
  
Functionality:  
- Protects the head from physical attacks  
- Repels or deflects incoming objects  
- May utilize existing manon fields to reduce mana consumption  
- Can convert mana into additional manon when required  
- Activates and deactivates at the user’s discretion  
  
Limitations:  
- Protects only the head  
- Requires conscious activation  
- Ineffective while inactive  
- Limited by available mana and manon  
- Extremely powerful impacts may exceed the medium’s defensive capacity  
- Provides little protection against hazards that cannot be influenced by manon  
  
Applications:  
- Personal defense  
- Thaumical combat  
- Protective equipment  
- Military gear  
- Hazardous environments
