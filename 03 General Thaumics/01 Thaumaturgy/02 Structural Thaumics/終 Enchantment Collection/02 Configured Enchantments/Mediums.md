Type: System  
  
Purpose:  
An enchantment designed to coordinate multiple [armana scripts](./Armana%20Script.md) toward a unified thaumical purpose.  
  
Principle:  
While a single engraving reproduces a singular thaumical function, a medium combines multiple scripts into an organized system. By distributing thaumical processes across specialized scripts, a medium may perform functions too complex, inefficient, or impractical for a single script alone.   
  
Structure:  
Consists of multiple armana scripts embedded within a stable physical substrate and arranged to exchange mana, information, or thaumical outputs with one another. The scripts collectively form a larger enchantment whose capabilities exceed those of its individual components.  
  
Functionality:  
- Coordinates multiple engravings  
- Executes multi-stage thaumical processes  
- Reduces cognitive burden on the user  
- Enables specialized or complex thaumical functions  
- Allows engravings to operate as a unified system  
- May automate portions of thaumical activity  
  
Limitations:  
- Requires greater construction complexity than individual engravings  
- Damage to critical engravings may impair overall functionality  
- Increased complexity generally increases mana consumption and maintenance requirements  
- Cannot exceed the capabilities of its constituent scripts  
  
Applications:  
- Thaumical tools  
- Weapons and armor  
- Personal augmentation systems  
- Defensive systems  
- Industrial thaumics  
- Ritual apparatus  
- Educational devices
