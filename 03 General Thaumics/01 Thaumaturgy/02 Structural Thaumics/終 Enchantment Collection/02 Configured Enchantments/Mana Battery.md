Type: Mana Container  
  
Purpose:    
An enchantment designed for the long-term storage and controlled transfer of [raw mana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md).  
  
Principle:    
The enchantment uses structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) to suspend the decay of raw mana within a stable containment configuration. Regulated release pathways allow stored mana to be transferred into external thaumical systems.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of a cohesive armana reservoir with configured intake, containment, and discharge structures. Higher-capacity batteries require greater structural cohesion to withstand mana pressure.  
  
Functionality:  
- Stores raw mana for extended durations  
- Prevents natural mana decay during containment  
- Transfers mana into connected enchantments or vessels  
- May operate as a stationary or portable mana source  
  
Limitations:  
- Large mana reservoirs are highly volatile  
- Structural failure may result in uncontrolled mana discharge  
- Excessive mana density can destabilize armana cohesion  
  
Applications:  
- Thaumical infrastructure  
- Portable mana supply  
- Enchantment power systems  
- Emergency mana reserves
