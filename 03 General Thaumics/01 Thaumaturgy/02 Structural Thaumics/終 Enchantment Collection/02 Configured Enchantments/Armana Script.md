Type: Fundamental  
  
Purpose:    
A foundational enchantment designed to perform a singular thaumical function through the structured arrangement of [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md).  
  
Principle:    
As armana structures approach limited aspects of the [mind’s](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.02%20Mind.md) complexity, they may reproduce specific mana-manipulating behaviors. A script encodes a predetermined thaumical process into a stable armana configuration, allowing that process to be executed without conscious calculation by the user.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of structured armana arranged to reproduce a singular thaumical operation. Scripts may be embedded within vessels, tools, weapons, armor, frameworks, or other stable physical substrates. Multiple scripts may be combined to form larger enchantments known as @mediums.  
  
Functionality:  
- Executes a predefined thaumical operation  
- Converts mana into a configured effect  
- Reduces the cognitive burden of thaumical manipulation  
- Allows rapid activation of encoded functions  
- Serves as a component of larger enchantment systems  
  
Limitations:  
- Performs only its encoded function  
- Possesses no awareness or decision-making capability  
- Requires structural integrity to operate correctly  
- Damage may reduce efficiency or disable functionality  
- Greater complexity increases construction and maintenance requirements  
  
Applications:  
- Spell execution  
- Personal augmentation  
- Weapon enchantments  
- Defensive systems  
- Utility thaumics  
- Components of mediums
