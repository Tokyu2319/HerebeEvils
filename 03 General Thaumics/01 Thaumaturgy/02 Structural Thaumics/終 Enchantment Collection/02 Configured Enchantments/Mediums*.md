Type: Interface  
  
Purpose:    
An enchantment designed to assist a being in the conversion, shaping, and manipulation of [mana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md) during thaumical activity.  
  
Principle:    
The enchantment provides preconfigured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) structures that perform portions of the cognitive and thaumical processes normally carried out by the user’s [mind](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.02%20Mind.md). By externalizing these functions, the user may perform thaumics with reduced concentration, greater consistency, or increased efficiency.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of configured armana pathways embedded within a physical medium such as a wand, staff, tome, focus crystal, or other object. The physical object serves as a stable substrate upon which the enchantment is maintained.  
  
Functionality:  
- Assists mana conversion processes  
- Guides manon projection and manipulation  
- Stabilizes thaumical structures during casting  
- Reduces cognitive burden on the user  
- May provide specialized thaumical functions depending on configuration  
  
Limitations:  
- Cannot perform thaumics without mana input  
- Effectiveness depends on enchantment complexity and user compatibility  
- Damage to the physical substrate may disrupt enchantment integrity  
- Excessive thaumical load may exceed the medium’s configured capacity  
  
Applications:  
- Wands  
- Staves  
- Tomes  
- Ritual implements  
- Thaumical tools  
- Educational casting aids
