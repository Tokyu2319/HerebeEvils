Thaumical procedures performed through the manipulation and conversion of [mana](./01%20Mana.md) by a [being](../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md), [enchantment](../02.02%20Structural%20Thaumics/01%20Enchantments.md), or other thaumical system.  
  
Spells differ in structure, complexity, required knowledge, mana expenditure, and practical accessibility. While some spells are commonplace and widely taught, others require rare understanding, specialized [vessels](../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md), or thaumical conditions beyond the capabilities of most civilizations.  
  
Spell classifications are not strictly determined by power, but by the rarity, sophistication, and practicality of their use within thaumical societies.  
  
# Elementary Spells  
Spells simple enough to be learned, reproduced, and maintained by most thaumical practitioners with basic education and vessel development.  
  
Elementary spells generally rely upon direct mana conversion or uncomplicated [manon](./03%20Manon.md) manipulation, requiring minimal precision, low configurational complexity, and manageable mana costs. These spells commonly form the foundation of thaumical instruction and daily utility.  
  
Due to their accessibility and reliability, elementary spells are widespread throughout thaumical civilizations.  
  
# Intermediate Spells  
Spells requiring greater precision, environmental awareness, or thaumical understanding than elementary spells.  
  
Intermediate spells often involve layered mana processes, sustained control, specialized interactions with matter or energy, or coordinated manon behavior. While broadly achievable by trained practitioners, they demand discipline and experience beyond foundational thaumics.  
  
Such spells are commonly associated with professional thaumists, military applications, industrial systems, or specialized academic study.  
  
# Esoteric Spells  
Spells possessing knowledge, mechanisms, or requirements inaccessible to the majority of practitioners or civilizations.  
  
Esoteric spells frequently depend upon rare theoretical understanding, unusual environmental conditions, specialized vessels, uncommon enchantments, or highly unstable thaumical processes. Their replication is often difficult due to incomplete documentation, extreme precision requirements, or the rarity of individuals capable of performing them.  
  
Many esoteric spells are confined to isolated traditions, research institutions, ancient systems, or singular practitioners.  
  
# Exceptional Spells  
Spells whose execution exceeds the practical or theoretical limits normally achievable within known thaumical systems.  
  
Exceptional spells may involve unprecedented mana scales, extraordinary configurational complexity, vessel-defying processes, reality-altering interactions, or thaumical phenomena not fully understood even by advanced civilizations.  
  
Such spells are exceedingly rare and may only exist as singular events, experimental constructs, legendary techniques, or impossible-to-reproduce phenomena.
