Type: Interface  
  
Purpose:    
An enchantment designed to integrate thaumical functions directly with a [being’s](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md) [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md) through embedded [armana](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) structures.  
  
Principle:    
The enchantment is anchored to a physical substrate located on or within a vessel. By maintaining proximity to the user’s natural [mana](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md) pathways, the engraving may assist, modify, or automate thaumical processes with minimal conscious effort.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of configured armana patterns embedded within a stable physical medium. The patterns are commonly arranged as markings, inscriptions, implants, or tattoo-like structures upon the vessel.  
  
Functionality:  
- Assists thaumical manipulation  
- Reduces casting complexity  
- Allows rapid activation of configured effects  
- Maintains persistent thaumical functions  
- May interface directly with vessel processes  
  
Limitations:  
- Requires continuous structural integrity  
- Damage to the engraving may impair functionality  
- Excessive complexity increases maintenance requirements  
- Prolonged operation may increase mana consumption  
  
Applications:  
- Personal thaumical enhancement  
- Defensive enchantments  
- Combat augmentation  
- Sensory enhancement  
- Utility thaumics
