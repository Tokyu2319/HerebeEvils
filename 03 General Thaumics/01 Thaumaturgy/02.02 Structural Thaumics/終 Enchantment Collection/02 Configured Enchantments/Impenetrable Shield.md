Type: Defense  
  
Purpose:    
An enchantment designed to prevent the passage of matter, energy, and thaumical effects through a protected boundary.  
  
Principle:    
The enchantment maintains a continuous [armana](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) structure around a designated region. Incoming interactions are opposed through the application of [manon](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/03%20Manon.md), preventing penetration of the barrier regardless of attack type.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of a persistent armana boundary supplied by a continuous mana source. The barrier may be anchored to a location, object, or [being](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md).  
  
Functionality:  
- Blocks physical impacts  
- Resists thermal and electromagnetic energy transfer  
- Restricts chemical intrusion  
- Impedes direct thaumical manipulation across the boundary  
- Provides continuous protection while supplied with mana  
  
Limitations:  
- Requires constant mana expenditure  
- Large protected volumes increase operational cost  
- Sustained attacks may overwhelm available mana supply  
- Barrier failure results in immediate loss of protection  
  
Applications:  
- Personal defense  
- Fortifications  
- Secure containment  
- Hazard isolation  
- Thaumical Combat
