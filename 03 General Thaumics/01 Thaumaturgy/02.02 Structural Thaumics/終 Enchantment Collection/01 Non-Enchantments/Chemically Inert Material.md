Type: Material Substitute  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to reproduce the structural properties of reactive or corrosive materials without reproducing their energetic chemical behavior.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) reproduces the structural and mechanical properties of matter through cohesive configuration rather than energetic atomic interactions. Because armana lacks intrinsic energy, chemically reactive processes such as oxidation, combustion, corrosion, and electrochemical reactions cannot occur within the structure.  
  
As a result, chemically inert materials may imitate the appearance and mechanical behavior of reactive substances while remaining chemically stable under environmental exposure.  
  
Structure:    
Chemically inert materials are formed through armana configurations reproducing the structural arrangements of conventional materials while lacking the energetic states required for chemical reactivity. High-continuity cohesion is required to maintain stable material behavior across exposed surfaces.  
  
Functionality:  
- Resists corrosion and oxidation  
- Maintains structural stability under chemical exposure  
- Prevents combustion and reactive degradation  
- Preserves material appearance over extended durations  
- Allows stable replication of otherwise reactive materials  
  
Limitations:  
- Cannot reproduce energetic chemical processes  
- Cannot function as fuel, propellant, or explosive material  
- Cannot support electrochemical energy generation or storage  
- Structural degradation may still occur through mechanical or thaumical damage  
  
Applications:  
- Corrosion-resistant infrastructure  
- Chemical storage systems  
- Marine structures  
- Industrial machinery  
- Long-term structural reinforcement  
- Lightweight metallic substitutes  
- Environmental protection systems
