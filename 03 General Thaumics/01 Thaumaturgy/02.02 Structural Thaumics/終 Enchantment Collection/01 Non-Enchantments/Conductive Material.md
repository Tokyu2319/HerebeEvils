Type: Material Substitute  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to reproduce the conductive properties of electrically conductive materials for power transfer, circuitry, and electromagnetic applications.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) reproduces the lattice configurations and charge-transfer pathways of conductive matter, allowing electrical current to propagate through the structure despite armana’s lack of intrinsic energy.  
  
Because conductivity emerges from structural permissiveness rather than energetic reaction, conductive armana may transmit electricity without reproducing the electrochemical behaviors of conventional conductive materials.  
  
Structure:    
Conductive materials are formed through cohesive armana arrangements replicating conductive crystalline and metallic lattice structures. High-continuity configurations are required to minimize decoherence and interruption of charge flow.  
  
Functionality:  
- Conducts electrical current  
- Transfers electrical energy across structures  
- Supports circuitry and electromagnetic systems  
- Allows lightweight conductive infrastructure  
  
Limitations:  
- Cannot generate electricity intrinsically  
- Cannot reproduce electrochemical reactions  
- Structural discontinuities interrupt conductivity  
- Damaged sections may decohere into bonded armana  
  
Applications:  
- Electrical wiring  
- Power distribution  
- Electromagnetic systems  
- Lightweight circuitry  
- Industrial machinery  
- Thaumic-technological infrastructure
