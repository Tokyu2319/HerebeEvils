Type: Material Substitute  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to provide structurally stable low-mass materials for transportation, mobility, and lightweight construction.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01.01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/04%20Armana.md) reproduces the structural properties of physical materials while lacking much of the intrinsic energy contributing to the mass of conventional matter. As a result, armana-based materials possess significantly reduced density while retaining useful mechanical properties.  
  
Structure:    
Light materials are formed through cohesive armana configurations reproducing the structural arrangements of lightweight conventional materials such as metallic lattices, composite frameworks, and crystalline structures. Reinforcement patterns are commonly incorporated to compensate for reduced inertial stability.  
  
Functionality:  
- Reduces structural mass  
- Improves buoyancy and mobility  
- Lowers transport and lifting requirements  
- Enables large lightweight constructions  
- Allows temporary deployment of oversized structures  
  
Limitations:  
- Reduced mass may decrease stability under strong external forces  
- Large lightweight structures are vulnerable to cohesion failure  
- Cannot reproduce energetic material behaviors  
- Excessive structural discontinuity may trigger decoherence  
  
Applications:  
- Ships  
- Aircraft  
- Airships  
- Portable structures  
- Mobility equipment  
- Lightweight tools  
- Deployable platforms  
- Structural frameworks
