Type: Manipulation / Illusion  
  
Description:    
The application of [manon](../../01%20Mana.md) to curve light and/or [phana](../../02%20Phana.md).  
  
Mechanism:    
Manon particles are projected from the user’s [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) to apply mechanical influence on light, redirecting its propagation around a target.  
  
Knowledge Requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon and phana  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Awareness of manon’s micro manipulation  
- Understanding of light and/or phana  
  
Use Cases:  
- Optical concealment  
- Stealth  
- Visual deception  
  
Limitations:    
- Effectiveness depends on the user’s competency in manon agency.    
- Force output scales with manon density.    
- Susceptible to disruption by external controlled manon fields, particularly when the area of influence is large or sustained over distance  
  
Notability:    
Represents the extension of manon manipulation into electromagnetic phenomena, allowing indirect concealment through the redirection of light rather than suppression of presence.
