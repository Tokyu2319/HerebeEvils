Teleportation magic is the process of instant travel from one point to the other using magic or enchantments.  
# The Methods on How to Teleport  
- ## Digital Teleportation  
	The most simplest method of teleportation is when a being takes on a digital [vessel](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md), this **doesn’t require magic** except for the initial [soul bind](./Soul%20Bind%20Magic*.md). Additionally, artificial beings born from digital vessels won’t require soul bind magic. How a digital being moves depends on the technology they’re using, if they’re using the internet to move they’ll be bound to the internet but they’d travel as fast as the internet.  
- ## Incorporeal Teleportation  
	An incorporeal being will be able to move as fast as light but will require an immense amount of mana. This makes the illusion that they are teleporting.  
- ## Conventional Teleportation  
	Conventional teleportation uses two distant enchantments where the armana structure is designed so it takes the biometric information of the being’s vessel (assuming the being is an organism) it then destroys the vessel and keeps it in storage for later use. Once the vessel is destroyed the being’s soul and mind are bound to an armana vessel using soul bind magic. The enchantment will now move the armana vessel following a path using manon to take it to the other enchantment which will then arrange the being’s vessel based on the biometric information it took, then it once again uses soul bind magic to the transported being into their newly created vessel.  
	Summary:  
	- The enchantment needs a supply of biological matter on both ends in order to accommodate multiple passengers and their mass.  
	- The enchantment needs to scan the biometric information of the passenger.  
	- The enchantment needs a path to use for transport.  
	- The enchantment needs to use soul bind magic for boarding and vessel establishment.
