Materialization is the process by which mana is converted into matter.  
  
Matter is formed directly on the surface of a [vessel](../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md), whether that surface lies within or outside the vessel’s physical boundary. Because materialization occurs at the vessel’s surface, the resulting matter is initially unstable unless properly defined and sustained.  
  
The creation of matter produces energy as a byproduct. This byproduct may be reduced through an understanding of material science and skilled manipulation of [manon](./01.01%20Mana.md), which assists in stabilizing the resulting structure.  
  
Fluids are comparatively easy to materialize, as they may flow outward from the vessel without requiring rigid structural continuity. Solids are more difficult, as they must be given stable form, cohesion, and boundary integrity.  
  
# Vessel Extension  
  
Vessel Extension is the act of expanding the vessel’s defined boundary through mana, allowing external matter, another vessel, or materialized matter to be treated as part of the user’s own vessel.  
  
This process is essential to creation magic, healing magic, and any thaumic act that requires stabilized integration between a being and newly formed matter.  
  
Without Vessel Extension, materialized matter may fail to remain structurally unified, especially in the creation of solids.  
  
# Materialization Techniques for Solid Matter  
  
## Extrusive Creation  
Extrusive Creation is the formation of matter by extruding it outward from a vessel.  
  
- During this process, Vessel Extension is used simultaneously so the newly formed matter is defined as part of the vessel.  
  
Example: creating a copper rod from one’s hands.  
  
## Laminative Creation  
Laminative Creation is the formation of matter by layering it gradually within or around a vessel.  
  
- This method is especially effective in fluid environments, where matter can accumulate in stable layers before solidifying.  
  
Example: creating food or nutrients within the stomach’s fluids, or forming copper layer by layer within a fluid medium.

# Different Types of Healing Magic  
Healing magic are separated on how complicated they are and how much biology knowledge you need starting with archaic healing magic to restoration magic.  
## Archaic Healing Magic  
Archaic healing magic is the creation of nutrients. Mana share can allow the user to provide healing to another whether they’re a plant or an animal. Archaic healing magic if performed carefully, may provide enough food and water for a being to last days without actually consuming any, rather than burning rations they’re burning their own thread of life to extend their survival, quite ironic.  
  
## Healing Magic  
Healing magic is the creation of new tissue. Used to close flesh wounds, bone fractures, and other damages of non-complicated organs. Caution is advised when using healing magic as you can accidentally give yourself cancer if you’ve no knowledge on your own physiology.  
  
## Restoration Magic  
Restoration magic is the creation of new organs and limbs. Complete knowledge on anatomy and physiology is needed in order to safely use restoration magic. New limbs can be created in an extrusive manner or be created inside a pressure bladder, a sealed membrane filled with fluids (see [03 Enchantments](03%20Enchantments.md)).
