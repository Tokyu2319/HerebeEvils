Mana is a fundamental thaumic particle that exists as both a resource and a universal force. It is the primary medium through which beings interact with reality beyond conventional physical processes.  
  
Mana is inherently transformative and may convert into multiple specialized states or configurations. The principal forms are:  
  
- [Phana](./01.02%20Phana.md)  
- [Manon](./01.03%20Manon.md)  
- [Armana](./01.04%20Armana.md)  
- [Matter](./01.05%20Materialization.md)  
  
All forms of mana originate from the same underlying substance and differ only in behavior, stability, and interaction.  
  
Mana exists both within and outside of beings.  
  
Within a [being](../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md), mana is generated and stabilized by the [soul](../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.03%20Soul.md). Outside the soul, mana gradually destabilizes and undergoes decay.  
  
---  
# Raw Mana  
Raw mana refers to mana particles existing independently from the soul and outside specialized conversion states.  
  
Raw mana naturally amplifies nearby mana interactions and may intensify ongoing thaumic phenomena when sufficiently concentrated.  
  
Because raw mana lacks stabilization, it is inherently temporary and subject to mana decay.  
  
---  
# Raw Mana Decay  
Raw mana generated by the soul remains stable only while contained within it.  
  
Once raw mana exits the soul, it cannot naturally return and begins undergoing irreversible decay. The rate of decay appears fixed and cannot be accelerated through known means.  
  
Upon complete decay, raw mana releases its remaining energy through several known decay manifestations:  
  
- radiant flashes and thermal discharge,  
- transient force reactions,  
- or spontaneous materialization.  
  
These manifestations correspond behaviorally to phana, armana, manon and materialization respectively.  
  
Armana may prolong the stability of mana and delay decay through [enchantments](../02%20Structural%20Thaumics/02.01%20Enchantments.md).
