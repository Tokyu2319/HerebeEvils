Type: Matter Materialization  
  
Description:  
The conversion of [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) into solid matter, created from the user’s [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) either internally or externally.  
  
Mechanism:  
Mana is converted into matter and organized into stable, rigid structures. Due to the structural constraints of solids, techniques such as [laminative creation](../../01.05%20Materialization.md) or [extrusive creation](../../01.05%20Materialization.md) are required to maintain integrity during formation.  
  
Knowledge Requirements:  
- Understanding of mana conversion into matter  
- Understanding of [mana share](../../01.01%20Mana.md)  
  
Use Cases:  
- Creation of tools, structures, or weapons  
- Creation of precious materials such as gold  
- Nutrient satiation (insoluble or structured)  
  
Limitations:  
- Requires greater precision and structural knowledge; instability or collapse may occur if internal arrangement is incorrect.  
  
Notability:  
Demonstrates the increased complexity of rigid matter creation, requiring deliberate structural organization and specialized techniques to achieve stability.
