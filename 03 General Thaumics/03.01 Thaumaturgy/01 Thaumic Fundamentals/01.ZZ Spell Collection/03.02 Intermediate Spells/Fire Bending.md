Type: Manipulation  
  
Description:    
The application of [manon](../../01.01%20Mana.md) to induce combustion by accelerating gaseous matter and heating its constituent particles until ignition conditions are met.  
  
Mechanism:    
Manon particles are projected from the user’s [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) to agitate air molecules at high intensity, increasing local temperature and pressure. When sufficient heat is produced in the presence of oxygen and combustible material, combustion is initiated. Sustained control allows the user to shape, direct, and maintain the resulting flame.  
  
Knowledge requirements:  
  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Understanding of combustion, heat transfer, and ignition conditions  
  
Use Cases:  
  
- Igniting flammable material  
- Producing and directing flame  
- Heating an area rapidly  
- Controlling combustion-based attacks  
  
Limitations:    
Force output scales with manon density    
Requires available oxygen and combustible material for sustained flames    
Susceptible to disruption by external manon under another user’s control, particularly when the area of influence is large or sustained over distance.  
  
Notability:    
A conceptual limitation where the user interprets force application as “elemental control,” when it is actually the manipulation of heat, air movement, and combustion conditions. As such, Fire Bending is effectively Air Bending with additional thermal and chemical constraints.
