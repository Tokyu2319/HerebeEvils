Interpreted from [Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frieren)  
  
Type: Projection / Disruption  
  
Description:  
The projection of a stabilized [armana](../../01.04%20Armana.md) construct containing concentrated [phana](../../01.02%20Phana.md) and propelled through [manon](../../01.03%20Manon.md) manipulation. The spell inflicts damage through a combination of high-velocity impact and energetic discharge upon contact.  
  
Mechanism:  
A simple armana structure is formed to act as a containment vessel for a concentrated phana payload. The structure is intentionally minimal, serving only to maintain cohesion and containment while in flight. Due to its sparse construction, the armana remains partially translucent, allowing the contained Phana to illuminate the projectile from within.  
  
Once formed, the construct is accelerated through manon manipulation to high velocity. Unlike projected manon fields, the projectile possesses its own structural cohesion and therefore remains stable without requiring continuous force application from the caster.  
  
Upon impact, the projectile transfers kinetic energy into the target through rapid momentum exchange. Simultaneously, the destabilization of the armana containment releases the stored phana, causing additional energetic damage in the affected area.  
  
The destructive capability of Zoltraak is therefore derived from both the projectile’s velocity and the energy contained within it.  
  
Knowledge Requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) conversion  
- Understanding of armana bonding  
- Understanding of Manon propulsion  
- Understanding of momentum and high-speed impact phenomena  
- Knowledge of stable multi-aspect mana configurations  
  
Use Cases:  
- Long-range combat  
- Penetrating armor through kinetic force  
- Delivering concentrated energetic payloads  
- Engaging mobile targets at distance  
- General-purpose thaumical warfare  
  
Limitations:  
- Projectile velocity is limited by available Manon output  
- Armana containment must remain stable during acceleration and flight  
- Insufficient velocity may reduce penetration effectiveness  
- Excessive acceleration may destabilize poorly constructed projectiles  
- Susceptible to interception by barriers and defensive [enchantments](../../../02%20Structural%20Thaumics/02.01%20Enchantments.md)  
  
Notability:  
A highly influential combat spell notable for integrating armana containment, phana payloads, and manon propulsion into a standardized projectile system. Its effectiveness arises from the combination of mechanical and energetic damage, making it one of the most versatile forms of offensive thaumics.
