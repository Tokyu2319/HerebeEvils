Type: Manipulation / Illusion  
  
Description:  
The application of [manon](../../01.03%20Manon.md) to refract light and/or structure emitted [phana](../../01.02%20Phana.md) into perceived images.  
  
Mechanism:  
Manon particles are projected from the user’s [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md) to manipulate the propagation of incoming light or emitted phana, constructing optical forms through controlled refraction, redirection, and phana alignment.  
  
Knowledge Requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon and phana  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Awareness of manon’s micro manipulation  
- Understanding of light and/or phana  
- Understanding of optical refraction and visual perception  
  
Use Cases:  
- Visual deception  
- False targets  
- Camouflage  
- Decoys  
- Projected symbols or messages  
  
Limitations:    
- Image clarity depends on the user’s competency in manon agency.  
- Images lack physical substance.  
- Refraction-based images are dependent on environmental lighting conditions.  
- Phana-supported images increase mana consumption.  
- Convincing motion and perspective become increasingly difficult at wider viewing angles.  
- Stable moving images require [enchantments](../../../02%20Structural%20Thaumics/02.01%20Enchantments.md).  
- multi-observer projections require enchantments and greater manon distribution and phana output.  
- Susceptible to disruption by external controlled manon fields, particularly when the area of influence is large or sustained over distance  
    
Notability:  
Represents the constructive application of optical manipulation, combining manon-directed refraction with structured phana emission to create perceived imagery.
