Type: Manipulation  
  
Description:  
The application of [manon](../../01.01%20Mana.md) to exert directional force and torque on objects, especially at a micro scale.  
  
Mechanism:  
Manon particles are projected from the user’s [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) to apply mechanical influence on objects.  
  
Knowledge requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Awareness of manon’s micro manipulation  
  
Use Cases:  
- Lifting and moving objects  
- Manipulating the inner workings of mechanism without specialized tools  
- Microscopic force application such as exciting air  
  
Limitations:  
Force output scales with manon density  
Susceptible to disruption by external manon under another user’s control, particularly when the area of influence is large or sustained over distance.  
  
Notability:  
Marks the evolution of telekinesis into a precision discipline, enabling interaction with internal and microscopic structures; however, it requires extreme control over manon, as minor instability can cause unintended damage at the point of application.
