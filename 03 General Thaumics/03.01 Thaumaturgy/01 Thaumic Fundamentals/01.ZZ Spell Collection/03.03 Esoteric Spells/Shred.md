Type: Manipulation / Disruption  
  
Description:  
  
The application of [manon](../../01.01%20Mana.md) at the moment of physical contact to produce localized tearing, slicing, or shearing damage through instantaneous force generation.  
  
Mechanism:  
Rather than projecting a manon field into a target as seen in [Rupture](./Rupture.md), Shred forms and releases manon directly at the point of contact. The generated force is applied immediately upon manifestation, producing concentrated shearing stresses that tear or separate material along the contact surface.  
  
Because the manon field exists only momentarily before force application, there is little opportunity for detection, disruption, or interference by external manon fields. However, this also prevents the spell from operating effectively beyond direct physical reach.  
  
Shred relies on principles derived from [Advanced Telekinesis](./Advanced%20Telekinesis.md), particularly rapid force generation and precise contact-based manipulation.  
  
Knowledge Requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Awareness of manon’s force application capabilities  
- Understanding of shearing forces and material failure  
  
Use Cases:  
- Enhancing strikes with tearing or cutting effects  
- Damaging armor at points of contact  
- Severing materials through concentrated shearing stress  
- Close-quarters combat where rapid casting is required  
  
Limitations:  
- Requires direct contact or extremely close proximity  
- Less effective for deep internal damage than Rupture  
- Force output scales with manon density  
- Cannot readily bypass barriers without first establishing contact  
  
Notability:  
A Rare Spell valued by close-combat practitioners. While less versatile than [Rupture](./Rupture.md), its near-instantaneous force application makes it difficult to anticipate or disrupt through counter-manipulation. Many users independently discover primitive forms of Shred before learning the more sophisticated techniques required for Rupture.
