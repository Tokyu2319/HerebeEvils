Type: Structural Formation  
  
Description:    
The temporary bonding of metastable [armana](../../01.04%20Armana.md) particles into simple cohesive structures.  
  
Mechanism:    
The user converts [mana](../../01.01%20Mana.md) into metastable armana particles before using [manon](../../01.03%20Manon.md) to stabilize interactions between nearby particles during their coherence window. Sufficient cohesion allows the particles to bond into complete structures.  
  
Knowledge Requirements:  
- Basic understanding of mana conversion into armana and manon  
- Basic understanding of armana bonding  
- Basic understanding of manon agency  
  
Use Cases:  
- Platforms  
- Simple tools  
- Melee weapons  
- Basic shields  
- Structural demonstrations  
  
Limitations:  
- Small structures possess limited cohesion   
- Insufficient bonding causes rapid decoherence and decay  
  
Notability:    
Serves as a foundational demonstration of armana bonding and cohesion formation.
