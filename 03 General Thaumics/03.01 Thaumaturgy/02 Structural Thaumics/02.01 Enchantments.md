Armana structures approaching the [complexities](02-the-principles-of-beings0104-complexity.md) of the [mind](02-the-principles-of-beings0102-mind.md) will reproduce the effects that the mind has on [mana](01-thaumic-fundamentals0101-mana.md), allowing mana conversion, manon control, and matter [materialization](01-thaumic-fundamentals0105-materialization.md). This complex type of armana is referred to as **programmed enchantments**, they are the secondary tool for the use of thaumics and they differ based on how intricate their design and purpose is.  
  
# Non-Enchantments  
Armana structures that don’t hold any complexity towards configuration. They serve to be simple shapes for a variety of uses. See [armana applications](01-thaumic-fundamentals#0104-armana.md#applications-without-the-mention-of-enchantments)
