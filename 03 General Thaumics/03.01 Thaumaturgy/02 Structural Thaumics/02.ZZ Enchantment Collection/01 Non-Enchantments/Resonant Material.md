Type: Material Substitute  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to transmit, amplify, dampen, or stabilize mechanical vibrations across structures.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.04%20Armana.md) may reproduce the elastic and vibrational properties of resonant materials through cohesive structural arrangements. By controlling structural continuity, density distribution, and internal reinforcement patterns, resonant materials may be optimized either for efficient vibrational transfer or vibrational suppression.  
  
Structure:    
Resonant materials are formed through highly continuous armana configurations designed to preserve oscillatory motion across the structure. Reinforcement geometries and layered cohesion patterns are commonly incorporated to regulate resonance propagation and prevent destabilizing structural feedback.  
  
Functionality:  
- Transfers mechanical vibrations  
- Amplifies or dampens resonance  
- Stabilizes oscillatory stress across structures  
- Reduces localized vibrational failure  
- Allows controlled acoustic and mechanical transmission  
  
Limitations:  
- Excessive resonance may destabilize armana cohesion  
- Structural discontinuities interrupt vibrational propagation  
- Poorly regulated resonance may propagate structural damage  
- Large oscillatory loads may induce decoherence  
  
Applications:  
- Acoustic structures  
- Vibration damping systems  
- Seismic stabilization  
- Industrial machinery  
- Communication systems  
- Resonance chambers  
- Structural reinforcement systems
