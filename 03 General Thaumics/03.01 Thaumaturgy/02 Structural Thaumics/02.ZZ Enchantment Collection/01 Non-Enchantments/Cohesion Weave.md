Type: Structural Reinforcement  
  
Purpose:    
Non-enchantments designed to reinforce large, flexible, or mobile armana structures through distributed cohesion support.  
  
Principle:    
Structured [armana](../../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.04%20Armana.md) relies on sufficient structural continuity to maintain persistence. Cohesion weaves distribute stress, stabilize local bonding failures, and reduce the propagation of decoherence across connected armana formations.  
  
Structure:    
Consists of interwoven armana reinforcement patterns embedded throughout a larger structure. These reinforcement networks commonly take the form of lattices, layered meshes, or branching cohesive pathways designed to maintain continuity between structural regions.  
  
Functionality:  
- Reinforces armana cohesion  
- Reduces localized structural failure  
- Improves flexibility without complete decoherence  
- Stabilizes large or mobile armana formations  
- Delays decay following partial structural damage  
  
Limitations:  
- Cannot restore fully collapsed structures  
- Severe discontinuity may overwhelm the reinforcement network  
- Increased structural complexity raises formation difficulty  
- Damaged weave regions may destabilize connected sections  
  
Applications:  
- Flexible armor  
- Large barriers  
- Airships  
- Mobile platforms  
- Structural frameworks  
- Deployable fortifications  
- Suspension systems
