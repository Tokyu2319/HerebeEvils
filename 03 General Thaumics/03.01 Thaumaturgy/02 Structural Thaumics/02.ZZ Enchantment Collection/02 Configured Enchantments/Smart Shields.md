Type: Defense  
  
Purpose:  
An enchantment designed to selectively intercept physical threats while minimizing mana consumption.  
  
Principle:  
The enchantment monitors surrounding conditions and activates defensive structures only when predefined criteria are satisfied. The shield remains inactive during normal conditions, reducing unnecessary mana expenditure.  
  
Structure:  
Consists of detection enchantments linked to rapidly deployable armana barriers. Threat evaluation structures determine whether activation conditions are met.  
  
Functionality:  
- Detects incoming physical threats  
- Deploys defensive barriers when triggered  
- Conserves mana during inactivity  
- May be configured with custom activation criteria  
- Can protect designated individuals or regions  
  
Limitations:  
- Ineffective against threats not recognized by its configuration  
- Detection delays may reduce effectiveness against extremely fast attacks  
- Vulnerable to misidentification and false triggers  
- Generally incapable of countering non-physical hazards  
  
Applications:  
- Personal defense equipment  
- Military armor  
- Automated security systems  
- Defensive infrastructure  
- Thaumical combat
