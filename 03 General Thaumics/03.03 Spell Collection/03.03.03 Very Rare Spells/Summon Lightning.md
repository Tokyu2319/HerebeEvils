Type: Manipulation / Disruption  
  
Description:  
The application of [manon](../../03.01%20Magic/01%20Mana.md) to induce electrical discharge within the atmosphere.  
  
Mechanism:  
Manon particles are projected from the user’s [vessel](../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) to destabilize the surrounding air, promoting charge separation and ionization. In suitable atmospheric conditions, this creates a conductive path for electrical discharge. Once lightning is formed, the caster may further guide or redirect the bolt toward a designated target.  
  
Knowledge requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../03.01%20Magic/01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Awareness of atmospheric charge behavior  
- Awareness of ionization and conductive discharge  
  
Use Cases:  
- Striking targets with directed lightning  
- Disrupting machinery or formations  
- Delivering high-intensity electrical damage  
  
Limitations:  
Highly dependent on environmental conditions  
Difficult or inefficient outside favorable atmospheric states  
Force output scales with manon density  
Susceptible to disruption by external manon under another user’s control, particularly when the area of influence is large or sustained over distance  
  
Notability:  
It requires advanced understanding of atmospheric behavior and the unusual application of manon to electrical discharge. Though often framed as “elemental control,” it is not a common expression of manon and depends on both specialized knowledge and precise execution.
