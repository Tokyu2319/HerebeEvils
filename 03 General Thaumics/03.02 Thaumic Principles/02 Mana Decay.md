[Mana](../03.01%20Magic/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.01%20Mana.md) generated by the [soul](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.03%20Soul.md) remains stable only while contained within it.  
  
Once mana exits the soul, it cannot naturally return and begins undergoing irreversible decay. The rate of decay appears fixed and cannot be accelerated through known means.  
  
Upon complete decay, mana releases its remaining energy in one of several forms:  
  
- radiant flashes and thermal discharge,  
- transient force reactions,  
- or spontaneous material conversion into heavy elements.  
  
[Armana](../03.01%20Magic/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.04%20Armana.md) may prolong the stability of mana and delay decay through [enchantments](03%20Enchantments.md).
