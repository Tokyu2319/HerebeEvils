Vessel extension is the act of expanding the defined boundary of a [vessel](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md) through [mana](../03.01%20Magic/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.01%20Mana.md), allowing external matter, another vessel, or materialized matter to be temporarily treated as part of the user’s own vessel.  
  
This principle is fundamental to many branches of thaumics:  
  
- [Materialization](../03.01%20Magic/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.05%20Materialization.md) relies upon Vessel Extension to stabilize newly materialized solid matter.  
  
- External @healing magic uses Vessel Extension to integrate damaged tissue into the caster’s vessel for reconstruction or restoration.  
  
- Advanced thaumic manipulation may use Vessel Extension to temporarily govern external structures, tools, or environments as though they were extensions of the vessel itself.  
  
Without Vessel Extension, materialized matter often lacks sufficient structural continuity to remain stable, especially in the creation of rigid solids or complex biological structures.
