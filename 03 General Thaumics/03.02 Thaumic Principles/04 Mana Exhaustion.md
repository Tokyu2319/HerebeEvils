[Mana](../03.01%20Magic/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.01%20Mana.md) exhaustion occurs when a being depletes its internal mana reserves beyond sustainable limits.  
  
Because mana contributes to the cohesion between the [vessel](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md), [mind](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.02%20Mind.md), and [soul](../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.03%20Soul.md), severe depletion may destabilize the integrity of the three Aspects themselves.  
  
Early symptoms commonly include:  
  
- physical weakness,  
- cognitive impairment,  
- emotional destabilization,  
- sensory distortion,  
- and diminished awareness of self.  
  
Extreme exhaustion may result in:  
  
- involuntary aspect separation,  
- collapse of cognitive continuity,  
- or death.  
  
Paradoxically, higher beings are often more vulnerable to catastrophic exhaustion despite their immense mana reserves. The vast scale of their reserves may obscure awareness of depletion until destabilization has already begun.
