Type: Manipulation / Disruption  
  
Description:  
The application of [manon](../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.01%20Mana.md) to directly inflict structural damage onto a target through concentrated internalized force application.  
  
Mechanism:  
Manon particles are projected from the user’s [vessel](../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01%20Beings.md) and embedded into a target at close range. Rather than exerting broad external force like Telekinesis, the manon field applies highly localized mechanical stress within the target’s structure. This may manifest as tearing, fracturing, bursting, shearing, or violent displacement depending on the method of force application.  
  
Rupture relies heavily on principles derived from [Advanced Telekinesis](./Advanced%20Telekinesis.md), particularly micro-scale force precision and internal force placement.  
  
Knowledge requirements:  
- Understanding of [mana](../../01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.01%20Mana.md) conversion to manon  
- Understanding of manon agency  
- Awareness of manon’s micro manipulation  
- Understanding of structural stress and internal force application  
  
Use Cases:  
- Causing internal fractures in rigid materials  
- Tearing organic tissue through localized force application  
- Destroying mechanisms from within  
- Bypassing external durability through internal stress concentration  
  
Limitations:  
Effective primarily at close or melee range due to instability and disruption of projected manon fields over distance  
Force output scales with manon density  
Susceptible to disruption by external manon under another user’s control  
  
Notability:  
A Very Rare Spell, as it depends on specialized knowledge of manon’s versatility and advanced precision control. Unlike more common manipulations, Rupture is not broadly accessible, since most users do not understand that manon can be applied in this manner, and even fewer can execute it reliably at close range.
