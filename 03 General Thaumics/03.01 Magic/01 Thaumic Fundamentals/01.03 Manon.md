Manon is a fundamental force-carrying particle responsible for directional and rotational interaction at both micro and macro scales. In its natural state, manon is metastable and rapidly decays into an isotropic burst of force.  
  
Under the agency of a mind or structured systems of [armana](./01.04%20Armana.md), manon enters a coherent state in which decay is temporarily suspended, allowing directed propagation and interaction.  
  
The magnitude and velocity of force generated by manon are proportional to the number of coherently synchronized particles under control.  
  
Controlled manon fields can destabilize or disrupt other fields governed by different minds.  
  
When uncontrolled manon briefly interacts with [raw mana](./01.01%20Mana.md) during its coherence window, it can catalyze the conversion of raw mana into additional manon. When controlled, manon fields are able to manipulate raw mana directly, instead of converting it into additional manon.  
  
Manon is the fundamental mediating substrate required for any structured manipulation of physical or thaumical processes. As such, listing its uses is less a matter of categorization and more an admission that every deliberate transformation—be it force, form, energy, or intent—inevitably passes through its domain.
