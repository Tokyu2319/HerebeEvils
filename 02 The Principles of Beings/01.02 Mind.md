The Mind is the second Aspect to emerge after the [vessel](0101-vessel.md), it is the awareness and consciousness of a being and the medium through which [experiences](02-experience.md) are interpreted and conveyed to the [soul](0103-soul.md).  
  
The Mind forms as the vessel develops a sufficient cognitive framework—the mechanisms capable of processing stimuli, forming patterns, and sustaining internal representation.  
  
For instance, a tree possesses basic cognition—reactive processes to light, water, and injury—yet this is insufficient to produce a true mind comparable to that of thinking organisms.  
  
The mind ceases to exist when its vessel is destroyed, which will bring about the soul entering the [transience](04-transience.md) state. If the mind itself is destroyed first, the vessel will provide a new mind when circumstances allow—one devoid of the prior [memories](03-memory.md) of its predecessor. Though this new consciousness is a separate individual, it is still the same soul.  
  
The mind is the primary tool for the use of thaumics with [armana](03-general-thaumics0301-thaumaturgy01-thaumic-fundamentals0101-mana.md) being the second as the mind is able to manipulate and convert [mana](03-general-thaumics0301-thaumaturgy01-thaumic-fundamentals0101-mana.md). The same way the mind manipulates mana, mana can manipulate the mind including its processes, see [Illusion Magic](03-general-thaumics0303-thaumic-collectionillusion-magic.md).
