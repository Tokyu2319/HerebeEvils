The Mind is the second Aspect to emerge after the [vessel](./01.01%20Vessel.md), it is the awareness and consciousness of a being and the medium through which [experiences](./02%20Experience.md) are interpreted and conveyed to the [soul](./01.03%20Soul.md).  
  
The Mind forms as the vessel develops a sufficient cognitive framework—the mechanisms capable of processing stimuli, forming patterns, and sustaining internal representation.  
  
For instance, a tree possesses basic cognition—reactive processes to light, water, and injury—yet this is insufficient to produce a true mind comparable to that of thinking organisms.  
  
The mind ceases to exist when its vessel is destroyed, which will bring about the soul entering the [transience](./03%20Transience.md) state. If the mind itself is destroyed first, the vessel will provide a new mind when circumstances allow—one devoid of the prior [memories](../04%20Perceptions%20on%20Reality/02%20Identity/02%20Memory*.md) of its predecessor. Though this new consciousness is a separate individual, it is still the same soul.  
  
The mind is the primary tool for the use of thaumics with [armana](../03%20General%20Thaumics/01%20Thaumaturgy/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md) being the second as the mind is able to manipulate and convert [mana](../03%20General%20Thaumics/01%20Thaumaturgy/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01%20Mana.md). The same way the mind manipulates mana, mana can manipulate the mind including its processes, see [Illusion Magic*](../03%20General%20Thaumics/%E7%B5%82%20Thaumic%20Collection/Illusion%20Magic*.md).
