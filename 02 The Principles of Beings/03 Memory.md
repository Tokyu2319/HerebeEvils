There’s two distinct types of memories, the [vessel](01-beings.md)’s memory and the [mind](01-beings.md)’s memory.  
  
The vessel’s memory is the capacity to store and recall information. connecting synapses and data in a hardware are information inside a vessel’s cognition.  
  
The mind’s memory is what I define as a being’s [identity](04-perceptions-on-reality03-identity.md). **During the process of interpreting [experiences](02-experience.md) the mind will form memories in itself as a byproduct**. These memories are separate from the vessel’s memories as they can be manipulated by [manon](03-general-thaumics0301-thaumaturgy01-thaumic-fundamentals0101-mana.md).  
  
The memories of both aspects are lost when those aspects are destroyed, naturally, the mind will die out when there’s no compatible vessel. **Recovering a soul’s mind and its memories is possible due to the existence of [resurrection magic](03-general-thaumics0303-thaumic-collectionresurrection-magic.md).**
