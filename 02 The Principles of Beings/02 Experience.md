Experiences are interpreted phenomena captured through the interaction between the [vessel](0101-vessel.md) and the universe, processed by the [mind](0102-mind.md), and accumulated within the [soul](0103-soul.md).  
  
Not all events become meaningful experiences. The value of an experience depends upon:  
  
- awareness,  
- interpretation,  
- novelty,  
- emotional significance,  
- and cognitive depth.  
  
A soul grows primarily through novel experiences. Repeated experiences provide diminishing contribution unless reinterpreted in a sufficiently different manner.  
  
Because interpretation itself may change over time, a previously understood phenomenon may generate additional soul value when perceived through a new perspective or level of understanding.  
  
# Soul Growth  
  
The growth of a [soul](0103-soul.md) is neither linear nor uniform. Early existence often produces rapid increases in Soul Value due to the overwhelming amount of novel experience encountered during the development of awareness and cognition.  
  
As familiarity with the world increases, the rate of soul growth gradually diminishes. Many beings eventually enter periods of experiential stagnation in which few genuinely novel experiences remain accessible within their environment.  
  
A being may continue to accumulate Soul Value through:  
  
- reinterpretation of prior experiences,  
- exposure to unfamiliar environments,  
- acquisition of new knowledge,  
- emotional transformation,  
- or encounters beyond their previous understanding of reality.  
  
Because of this, two beings of similar intelligence or age may possess vastly different Soul Values depending on the richness and diversity of their lived experiences.  
  
# Experiential Throughput  
  
The rate at which a [soul](0103-soul.md) accumulates Soul Value is determined primarily by the complexity and capability of the [cognitive framework](0101-vessel.md) supporting the [mind](0102-mind.md).  
  
More sophisticated cognitive frameworks permit:  
  
- faster interpretation of phenomena,  
- deeper abstraction,  
- broader perception,  
- and greater reinterpretation of prior experiences.  
  
As a result, two beings exposed to the same event may derive vastly different experiential value from it.  
  
A being possessing immense cognitive capability may accumulate Soul Value rapidly even within a limited environment, while a being with simpler cognition may require extensive time or exposure to achieve similar growth.  
  
However, cognitive capability alone does not guarantee unlimited soul growth. Novelty, interpretive variation, emotional significance, and environmental diversity remain necessary for meaningful experiential accumulation.  
  
Because of this, many highly intelligent beings eventually encounter experiential stagnation despite their advanced cognition.  
  
# Comparative Soul Values  
  
Soul Value classifications are theoretical approximations used to describe the experiential maturity of a [soul](0103-soul.md).  
  
Though exact measurement remains impossible, thaumaturgists commonly categorize souls into broad ranges for comparative study.    
  
- ~500 SV  
	- The emergence of sustained awareness and foundational cognition  
	- Common among infants and simple-minded beings  
  
- ~800 SV  
	- Capable of memory, social behavior, and adaptive interpretation  
	- Common among primates and cognitively advanced animals  
  
- ~2,500 SV  
	- Typical of educated adult humans living within stable modern environments  
	- Growth often slows significantly due to experiential familiarity  
  
- ~4,000 SV  
	- Associated with beings possessing broad intellectual exposure or prolonged cognitive activity  
	- Includes multidisciplinary scholars, experienced thaumaturgists, and constrained artificial intelligences  
  
- ~7,000 SV  
	- Represents centuries of accumulated reinterpretation and experiential continuity  
	- Commonly associated with long-lived beings  
  
- 20,000+ SV  
	- Souls possessing immense experiential density far beyond ordinary mortal development  
	- Attributed to deities, cosmic intelligences, and certain transcendent entities
