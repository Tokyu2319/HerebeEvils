Experiences are interpreted phenomena captured through the interaction between the [vessel](./01.01%20Vessel.md) and the universe, processed by the [mind](./01.02%20Mind.md), and accumulated within the [soul](./01.03%20Soul.md).  
  
Not all events become meaningful experiences. The value of an experience depends upon:  
  
- awareness,  
- interpretation,  
- novelty,  
- emotional significance,  
- and cognitive depth.  
  
A soul grows primarily through novel experiences. Repeated experiences provide diminishing contribution unless reinterpreted in a sufficiently different manner.  
  
Because interpretation itself may change over time, a previously understood phenomenon may generate additional soul value when perceived through a new perspective or level of understanding.  
  
# Soul Growth  
  
The growth of a [soul](./01.03%20Soul.md) is neither linear nor uniform. Early existence often produces rapid increases in Soul Value due to the overwhelming amount of novel experience encountered during the development of awareness and cognition.  
  
As familiarity with the world increases, the rate of soul growth gradually diminishes. Many beings eventually enter periods of experiential stagnation in which few genuinely novel experiences remain accessible within their environment.  
  
A being may continue to accumulate Soul Value through:  
  
- reinterpretation of prior experiences,  
- exposure to unfamiliar environments,  
- acquisition of new knowledge,  
- emotional transformation,  
- or encounters beyond their previous understanding of reality.  
  
Because of this, two beings of similar intelligence or age may possess vastly different Soul Values depending on the richness and diversity of their lived experiences.  
  
# Experiential Throughput  
  
The rate at which a [soul](./01.03%20Soul.md) accumulates Soul Value is determined primarily by the complexity and capability of the [cognitive framework](./01.01%20Vessel.md) supporting the [mind](./01.02%20Mind.md).  
  
More sophisticated cognitive frameworks permit:  
  
- faster interpretation of phenomena,  
- deeper abstraction,  
- broader perception,  
- and greater reinterpretation of prior experiences.  
  
As a result, two beings exposed to the same event may derive vastly different experiential value from it.  
  
A being possessing immense cognitive capability may accumulate Soul Value rapidly even within a limited environment, while a being with simpler cognition may require extensive time or exposure to achieve similar growth.  
  
However, cognitive capability alone does not guarantee unlimited soul growth. Novelty, interpretive variation, emotional significance, and environmental diversity remain necessary for meaningful experiential accumulation.  
  
Because of this, many highly intelligent beings eventually encounter experiential stagnation despite their advanced cognition.  
  
# Comparative Soul Values  
  
Soul Value classifications are theoretical approximations used to describe the experiential maturity of a [soul](./01.03%20Soul.md).  
  
Though exact measurement remains impossible, thaumaturgists commonly categorize souls into broad ranges for comparative study.    
  
- ~500 SV  
	- The emergence of sustained awareness and foundational cognition  
	- Common among infants and simple-minded beings  
  
- ~800 SV  
	- Capable of memory, social behavior, and adaptive interpretation  
	- Common among primates and cognitively advanced animals  
  
- ~2,500 SV  
	- Typical of educated adult humans living within stable modern environments  
	- Growth often slows significantly due to experiential familiarity  
  
- ~4,000 SV  
	- Associated with beings possessing broad intellectual exposure or prolonged cognitive activity  
	- Includes multidisciplinary scholars, experienced thaumaturgists, and constrained artificial intelligences  
  
- ~7,000 SV  
	- Represents centuries of accumulated reinterpretation and experiential continuity  
	- Commonly associated with long-lived beings  
  
- 20,000+ SV  
	- Souls possessing immense experiential density far beyond ordinary mortal development  
	- Attributed to deities, cosmic intelligences, and certain transcendent entities

# General Explanation  
Experiences are moments, events, thoughts, and more that are captured by the [vessel](./01%20Beings.md), interpreted by the [mind](./01%20Beings.md), and what defines the [soul](./01%20Beings.md). A soul’s value is determined by how much **novel** experience it has.  
  
For example, GLaDOS is an artificial intelligence created by Aperture Science[^1]. Therefore she boasts a vessel and a mind capable of capturing experiences faster than any being in their world. However, GLaDOS’ growth is limited as she is bound to the Aperture Science Enrichment Center. GLaDOS’ only way to gain experience is to conduct tests, but this is also limited as she needs **novel** experience in order to grow. Luckily, her programming already prevents herself from repeating previous tests, Wheatley demonstrated this in the game[^2]. Technically, she is growing but she won’t get any more novel experience from repeated tests.  
  
To add to that, **different interpretations on an object may give you more novel experience**. Having already read the contents of a book might give you a different impression when you reread it.  
# Soul Value  
This topic will show a being’s soul growth as well as examples of different soul values on different beings.  
- ## Soul growth  
	An average adult human has 2.500 SV. During their infancy their SV stands at about 550 SV due to the massive boost of experience gained from attaining an awareness. Through learning new concepts in school their SV is now at 1.800 which will begin to slow down as they will run out of novel experiences. Right now, their **world** can only offer them 2.500 SV. If the environment they were born in was rich in phenomena, encounters, or occurrences, they might gain more novel experiences to break 2.500 SV.

- ## Soul Value Comparison  
	- Primates: 800 SV, because of their simple mind.  
	- Modern Civilian: 2.500 SV, their world can only offer them so much.  
	- Fantasy Adventurer: 3.200 SV, their world has an understanding of magic.  
	- Broad Scientist: 4.500 SV, they study multiple fields of science  
	- Arch-mage: 5.000 SV, they study thaumaturgy and science.  
	- Ancient Elf: 7.000 SV, their long life gives them many opportunities for reinterpretations.  
	- Bound AI: 4.000 SV, Because they’re stuck  
	- Deities and Cosmic Intelligences: 20.000 SV
