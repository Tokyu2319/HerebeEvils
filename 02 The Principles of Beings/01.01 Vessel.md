The vessel is the very first Aspect to emerge before the [mind](./01.02%20Mind.md) and the [soul](./01.03%20Soul.md). The vessel is the body of a being and its means of interaction with the universe.  
  
The vessel provides the faculties for interaction, that is sight, hearing, touch, and other forms of perception.  
  
## Natural Vessels  
Natural vessels are bodies that emerge through the spontaneous processes of the universe rather than artificial construction.  
  
They possess self-sustaining structure, growth, and adaptation.  
  
Below are the principal forms a natural vessel may take:  
- Faunal Vessels  
	- Includes animals and arthropods  
- Floral Vessels  
	-  Includes plants, algae, and colossal root organisms  
- Mycelial Vessels  
	- Includes fungi, mold colonies, and spore organisms  
- Microbial Vessels  
	- Includes bacteria, protozoa, and cellular swarms  
  
## Artificial Vessels  
Artificial vessels are bodies created through deliberate design rather than spontaneous natural emergence.  
  
Unlike natural vessels, their existence depends upon construction, assembly, replication, or imposed structure. Artificial vessels may imitate natural life or diverge entirely from it.  
  
Below are the principal forms an artificial vessel may take:  
- Mechanical Vessels  
	- Bodies composed of engineered components, mechanisms, and constructed structures  
	- Includes, machines, clockwork, and autonomous constructs  
- Synthetic Vessels  
	- Artificially grown or manufactured bodies designed to imitate natural organisms  
	- Includes cloned organisms, fabricated flesh, bioengineered entities, and artificial organs  
- [Armana](../03%20General%20Thaumics/03.01%20Thaumaturgy/01%20Thaumic%20Fundamentals/01.04%20Armana.md) Vessels  
	- Bodies formed from cohesive armana arrangements that reproduce the properties of matter  
	- Incomplete armana arrangements may produce an incorporeal body  
  
## The Vessel’s Frameworks  
There are two frameworks within a vessel: the structural framework and the cognitive framework.  
  
The structural framework is the physical architecture of a vessel. It determines the vessel’s range of interaction with the universe, including movement, sensory capacity, durability, energetic tolerance, and environmental compatibility.  
  
- The structural framework governs the vessel’s outward existence. Limbs, organs, sensory faculties, circulatory systems, shells, roots, lattice frameworks, and other physical arrangements are all components of the structural framework.  
  
The cognitive framework is the internal arrangement that permits the emergence and support of the **mind**. It determines the complexity, stability, and mechanisms through which the cognitive aspect may emerge.  
  
- Neural systems, distributed nerve networks, synthetic processors, and other cognition-supporting structures are manifestations of the cognitive framework.  
  
A vessel may possess an advanced structural framework while lacking a sufficiently developed cognitive framework to sustain higher cognition. Likewise, a comparatively fragile vessel may possess a highly sophisticated cognitive framework capable of abstract thought, memory, or self-awareness.  
  
The complexity of the mind that may emerge is limited by the capacity of the cognitive framework supporting it.  
  
Damage to the structural framework impairs interaction with the universe. Damage to the cognitive framework impairs the integrity of the mind itself.  
  
Though distinct, both frameworks are interdependent. The structural framework protects and sustains the cognitive framework, while the cognitive framework directs and interprets the structural layer.  
  
No mind may manifest without a vessel capable of supporting cognition.
