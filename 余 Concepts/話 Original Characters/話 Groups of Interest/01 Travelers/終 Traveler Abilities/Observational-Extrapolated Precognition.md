User: The Precognitive Traveler  
  
Description:    
The ability to predict future events through complete observation of the present state of the user’s current [Region](../../../../../01%20The%20Laws%20of%20Reality/01%20The%20Universe.md). Rather than perceiving the future directly, the ability gathers information from reality and extrapolates the most probable outcomes from that information.  
  
The ability is divided into two separate mechanisms, Observation and Extrapolation, both of which require a resource named Rest.  
  
Mechanism:  
- Rest    
    - Rest is accumulated whenever the user enters a state of reduced consciousness, such as sleep, unconsciousness, or other similar conditions. Rest can only be generated while occupying a [Vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md) capable of genuine sleep. Vessels incapable of sleep, such as many fully artificial bodies, cannot generate Rest regardless of inactivity or shutdown duration.  
    - Rest is consumed whenever predictions are made, with expenditure scaling according to the complexity, duration, and specificity of the prediction.  
- Observation    
    - Observation continuously captures the present state of every object within the user’s current Region. Due to the overwhelming amount of information available, the ability automatically filters irrelevant data before relaying useful information to Extrapolation.  
    - The filtering process prioritizes information most relevant to the user’s circumstances, ensuring that only actionable data is processed. The user is not consciously aware of the information gathered by Observation.  
    - Observation cannot gather information from outside the user’s current Region. Any phenomena originating beyond the observed Region are treated as unknown information.  
- Extrapolation    
    - Extrapolation predicts future outcomes by analyzing information supplied by Observation. The accuracy of a prediction depends entirely on the completeness of the information provided.    
    - Since Observation gathers information from across an entire Region, Extrapolation is capable of producing predictions with extreme accuracy regarding events occurring within that Region.  
    - Extrapolation remains dependent on available information and cannot account for phenomena that are fundamentally unknowable from the present state of the observed Region.  
    - The farther a prediction extends into the future, the more Rest it requires to generate.  
  
Passive Ability:  
- Death Prediction    
    Imminent situations that would result in the user’s death are automatically predicted and relayed without conscious activation.    
    The warning is generated regardless of the source of danger, provided sufficient Rest is available.    
    Predictions where the user survives, even with severe injury or permanent disability, are generally ignored by the ability and are not relayed.  
    Death Prediction incorporates the predicted effects of its own warning into the extrapolation process. As a result, the ability only activates when the user’s death remains the most probable outcome even after accounting for the user’s expected response.  
  
Active Ability:  
- Desired Future    
    The user may consciously request predictions relating to a specific goal, action, or desired outcome.    
    Examples include identifying a sequence of actions that would lead to survival, locating a target, succeeding in a negotiation, or achieving another specified objective.    
    Desired Future predictions require substantially more Rest than passive predictions due to the increased complexity of searching for favorable outcomes among countless possibilities.  
  
Limitations:  
- Rest functions as a finite resource.  
- Rest can only be accumulated while occupying a Vessel capable of sleep.  
- Predictions become more expensive as their timeframe increases.  
- Desired Future predictions consume significantly more Rest than passive predictions.  
- Observation gathers present information only and cannot directly perceive future events.  
- Observation is limited to the user’s current Region and cannot gather information from other Regions.  
- Extrapolation remains dependent on available information and cannot account for phenomena that are fundamentally unknowable from the present state of the observed Region.  
- Predictions are not guarantees; they represent the outcome that Extrapolation expects based on the information it possesses.  
  
  
Notability:    
[Separation Anxiety Disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separation_anxiety_disorder) (SAD) is an [anxiety disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anxiety_disorder "Anxiety disorder") in which an individual experiences excessive anxiety regarding separation from home and/or from people to whom the individual has a strong emotional attachment.
