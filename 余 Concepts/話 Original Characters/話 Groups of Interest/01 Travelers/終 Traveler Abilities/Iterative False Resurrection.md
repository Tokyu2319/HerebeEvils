User: The Soul Traveler  
  
Description:    
A passive ability that preserves the continuity of the user’s [soul](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.03%20Soul.md) through repeated cycles of death and rebirth. Rather than restoring a lost [identity](../../../../../04%20Perceptions%20on%20Reality/02%20Identity/01%20Identity*.md) the ability creates a new identity upon activation while retaining the underlying Soul. Through the accumulation and consumption of stored [experiences](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/02%20Experience.md), each successive iteration possesses a larger and more mature soul despite the loss of its predecessor.  
  
The ability derives its name from the appearance of resurrection. While observers may perceive the user as returning from death, the previous identity is not restored. Instead, a new identity emerges upon the same soul.  
  
Mechanism:  
- Soul Collection    
	- Iterative False Resurrection allows the user to collect nearby [transient](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/03%20Transience.md) souls and store them within their own soul. Collection occurs subconsciously whenever a transient soul enters the ability’s effective range.  
	- Collection occurs within a limited area surrounding the user, typically extending only a few paces from their current position.  
	- Stored souls are normally reserved for future activations of Iterative False Resurrection. However, identities possessing sufficient thaumaturgical knowledge may interact with collected souls before they are consumed, allowing them to be utilized for other soul-based phenomena.  
- Experience Storage    
    - Experiences obtained from collected souls are stored separately from the user’s normal experiential development. These stored Experiences do not contribute to the current identity’s soul growth and remain inaccessible until activation of the ability.  
- Activation Condition    
    - Iterative False Resurrection activates automatically upon the death of the user or the total destruction of the [mind](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.02%20Mind.md). The ability cannot be consciously activated or suppressed.  
- Reconstitution    
    - Upon activation, the ability reconstructs a new [vessel](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/01.01%20Vessel.md) for the user’s soul. The resulting vessel is small in frame and resembles that of a juvenile individual.    
    - The newly formed Identity possesses only foundational knowledge necessary for function, such as language, basic cognition, and selective memories carried over from previous iterations. Most personal memories, relationships, and psychological traits associated with the prior Identity are not restored.  
- Experiential Consumption    
    - During reconstitution, all stored experiences accumulated by the ability are consumed and converted into soul growth. This includes experiences collected from stored Souls and experiences preserved from previous identities.  
	1. The resulting increase in [soul value](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/02%20Experience.md#Soul%20Growth) permanently strengthens the underlying soul before the emergence of the new Identity.  
- Forced Manifestation    
    - If environmental conditions prevent successful activation, the ability will alter local circumstances sufficiently to permit manifestation. Examples include:  
		- Fracturing surrounding matter if the activation site is physically obstructed.  
		- Displacing debris preventing Vessel formation.  
		- Creating sufficient space for reconstitution.  
  
	- This process is limited solely to enabling activation and does not otherwise grant control over the environment.  
  
Limitations:  
- Only Transient Souls may be collected.  
- Activation only occurs upon death or total destruction of the Mind.  
- The previous identity is permanently lost during activation.  
- Most memories are not retained between iterations.  
- Stored experiences are consumed during activation and cannot be recovered afterward.   
- New identities remain susceptible to [experiential stagnation](../../../../../02%20The%20Principles%20of%20Beings/02%20Experience.md#Experiential%20Throughput) despite inheriting a soul with immense soul value.  
- The ability does not prevent death and only functions after death has occurred.  
  
Notability:    
[Amnesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amnesia) is a deficit in memory caused by brain damage or brain diseases.
